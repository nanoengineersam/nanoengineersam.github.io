RESEARCH / NANOTECHNOLOGY

Nanotechnology — experimental work at the nanoscale.

Characterizing semiconductor nanostructures, impurity-related phenomena and nanoscale electronic properties using scanning probe and complementary microscopy techniques.

AFMSPMESMSEM / EDSRaman

AFM & SPM

Nanoscale surface morphology and local electronic properties are investigated with atomic force and scanning probe methods. This provides a bridge between physical structure and local electrical behavior.

Experiment

My experimental work focuses on silicon-based semiconductor systems, impurity diffusion and the formation of nanoscale structures associated with transition-metal impurities.

Nickel nanoclusters in silicon

The central research problem is the formation of nickel-related nanoclusters in silicon. Experimental observations are combined with microscopy, electrical characterization and atomistic calculations to understand the underlying mechanism.

From measurement to manipulation

A longer-term direction is to connect nanoscale characterization with controlled manipulation, precision positioning and improved scanning-probe capabilities.
