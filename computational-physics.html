RESEARCH / COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS

Computational Physics — from atomic models to electronic structure.

First-principles modeling of semiconductor materials, point defects, impurity atoms, electronic states and atomic interactions.

DFTQuantum ESPRESSOASEPython

DFT

Density functional theory is used to examine defect configurations, electronic structure, charge redistribution, spin polarization and interactions between impurity atoms in silicon.

Quantum ESPRESSO

Current calculations use Quantum ESPRESSO for plane-wave DFT simulations, with Python and ASE supporting model preparation and analysis.

Ni in silicon

The computational component of my research examines substitutional and interstitial nickel configurations, Ni–Si hybridization, defect states and the interaction responsible for impurity clustering.

Workflow

Model → calculate → converge → analyze → interpret. Typical analysis includes total energies, PDOS, charge density, magnetization and structural relationships.
